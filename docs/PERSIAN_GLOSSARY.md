# Persian label glossary

Every playable map node and proxy unit, with its English and Persian label. This is the review sheet for a native-speaker pass: the Persian names are transliterations of the briefs' place names, not sourced translations.

Conventions: Arabic hamza is kept in words such as تأسیسات; Hebrew place names follow Persian press usage (رمات داوید, هاتزور); Persian city forms are used instead of English transliterations (بندرعباس, خرم‌آباد).

## Open review points

- Hebrew base names (هاتزور, اوفدا, رمات داوید) have more than one accepted Persian transliteration.
- `پایگاه هوایی ثمریت` follows the Arabic spelling; some Persian outlets write `ثمرایت`.
- The proxy names use the familiar Persian forms حزب‌الله, حماس, حشد شعبی; no political labelling is implied.
